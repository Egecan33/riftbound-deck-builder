**Sequencing interactions found in this list**
- [ready-after] Master Yi, Tempered first, THEN First Mate: units enter exhausted (rule 359.2.c) and the Standard Move costs an exhaust (rule 144.2), so the readier must resolve second to buy a same-turn move. Master Yi, Tempered + First Mate = 7 Energy: turn 4 on the play, turn 3 on the draw; P(First Mate seen by then) = 37.2% / 33.1%. Other targets: Rengar, Trophy Hunter, Ruin Runner.
- [needs-body] Pit Rookie x3 want a friendly unit already on board (their play effect needs another unit): lead with Lonely Poro, Scuttle Crab, Fiora, Peerless.
- [pre-combat] Cast Charm x3, Sabotage x3 BEFORE the move that starts the showdown: they have neither Action nor Reaction, so they are unplayable in a Showdown State (rules 308.1.a, 310.1.a).
- [action-window] Punch First x3, Challenge x1, Primal Strength x1 are Action speed: playable only when you hold Focus in a showdown (rules 313.1, 347.1). As attacker you receive Focus first (rule 464.2.d), so passing with these in hand lets the defender close the showdown (rule 347.2.a); as defender you act after the attacker's first play.
- [reaction-hold] Defy x3, En Garde x2, Discipline x3 are Reaction speed: they can answer a chain item without Focus (rules 309.1.a, 312.2.d, 813.1.c). Cheapest hold needs 1 ready rune(s); runes are spent when the pool is filled, and the pool empties at end of turn (rule 167), so the runes must be left ready.
- [hidden] Hide Zhonya's Hourglass x2 the turn BEFORE you need it: hiding costs [A] (one rune recycled, rule 164.2.b), needs a battlefield you control with no facedown card, and the card gains Reaction at cost 0 only from the next turn (rule 811.1.b). Its targets must be at that battlefield (rule 811.1.d.2).
- [ambush] Rengar, Trophy Hunter x3: hold the runes and play during the showdown as a Reaction to a battlefield where you already have a unit (rule 822.1.b); the opponent assigns nothing to it before it arrives.
- [alone] One unit per battlefield: Lonely Poro, En Garde, Fiora, Peerless, Wuju Bladesman - Starter check 'alone / one on one'. Reinforcing a held battlefield turns these off; send the second unit to the other battlefield instead (two battlefields in 1v1, rule 485.4).
- [xp] Level thresholds in list: [6]. Best Hunt value 2 (Master Yi, Tempered): Level 6 needs 3 conquer/hold triggers from that unit (rule 823.1.c.1), i.e. not before that unit's scoring turn number 3; other XP sources: Scuttle Crab.
- [power-tax] Punch First x3 cost 2+ Power: each Power recycles a rune to the Rune Deck (rules 164.2.b, 161.2.b). Punch First on turn 3 on the play leaves 4 runes, so turn 4 has 6 instead of 8.
- [damage-heals] Challenge x1 deal damage, and all units heal in the Combat Cleanup (rule 466.1.a.1) and at end of turn (rule 317.2.b): stack them with combat damage inside the same showdown or finish the unit outright.
- [deathknell] Deathknell carriers Lonely Poro x3, Scuttle Crab x3: trade them or leave them as the lone defender on purpose; the trigger is the value.
