RIFTBOUND FORGE
===============

Build, rate and improve Riftbound TCG decks on your own computer.
Nothing to install: this folder carries its own copy of Node.js.

START
  macOS:   double-click "Riftbound Forge.command"
  Windows: double-click "Riftbound Forge.bat"

A window opens and your browser shows the app. Keep that window open while you use
Riftbound Forge. Close it, or press Ctrl+C in it, to stop.

FIRST RUN
  The first start downloads the card data, recent tournament and community deck lists,
  and about 1,100 card images (roughly 110 MB).
  - Card data and deck lists take a few minutes. The app opens as soon as they are done.
  - Images keep downloading in the background. Cards show without pictures until
    theirs arrive. If you stop the app early, the download picks up where it left off.
  After that, Riftbound Forge works offline. Refresh the data from the Data page.

IF YOUR COMPUTER BLOCKS IT
  macOS ("cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified developer"):
    right-click "Riftbound Forge.command", choose Open, then Open again. Only needed once.
    Or in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/path/to/this/folder"
  Windows ("Windows protected your PC"):
    click "More info", then "Run anyway".

WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES
  macOS:   ~/Library/Application Support/Riftbound Forge
  Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Riftbound Forge
  Your decks, settings, card data and images are all there, not in this folder.

UPDATING
  Download the new zip from
  https://github.com/Egecan33/riftbound-deck-builder/releases/latest
  unzip it, and start it the same way. You can delete the old folder. Your data is kept.
