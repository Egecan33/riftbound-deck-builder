Riftbound Forge builds, rates and improves Riftbound TCG decks on your own computer. These downloads include their own Node.js, so there is nothing else to install.

**Downloads**
- macOS Apple Silicon (M1 and later): `RiftboundForge-<version>-macos-arm64.zip`
- macOS Intel: `RiftboundForge-<version>-macos-x64.zip`
- Windows 10/11 (x64): `RiftboundForge-<version>-windows-x64.zip`

**First run**
1. Unzip and double-click `Riftbound Forge.command` (macOS) or `Riftbound Forge.bat` (Windows).
2. The first start downloads card data and meta deck lists (a few minutes, then the app opens in your browser) and card images in the background (about 110 MB).
3. macOS may say the developer is unidentified: right-click the file, choose Open, then Open again. Windows SmartScreen: More info, then Run anyway.

Your data is kept in `~/Library/Application Support/Riftbound Forge` (macOS) or `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Riftbound Forge` (Windows). To update, download the new zip; your data stays. See `READ ME FIRST.txt` in the zip.
